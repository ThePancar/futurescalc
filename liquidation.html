Liquidation Price Calculator

Estimate the liquidation price for an isolated-margin USDT perpetual position.

Formula

Long: entry × (1 − 1/leverage + MMR). Short: entry × (1 + 1/leverage − MMR). This is an approximation for isolated margin; exchanges also account for fees, tiered maintenance margin and mark price, so the real level may differ slightly.
