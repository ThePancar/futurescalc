PnL / ROE Calculator

Profit, loss and return on equity for a futures trade.

How it works

Notional = margin × leverage. Quantity = notional ÷ entry. PnL = (exit − entry) × quantity for longs (reversed for shorts). Fees are charged on entry and exit notional. ROE = net PnL ÷ margin.
