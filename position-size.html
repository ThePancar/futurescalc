Position Size Calculator

How big should your position be so a stop-loss hit costs exactly your chosen risk?

How it works

Risk amount = balance × risk %. Stop distance = |entry − stop| ÷ entry. Position size (notional) = risk amount ÷ stop distance. Required margin = notional ÷ leverage. Fees and slippage are not included.
